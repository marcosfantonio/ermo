# Audio sources

- `door_open.wav` and `door_close.wav`: Rudmer_Rotteveel, “Creaky Door Slow 01” and “Creaky Door Closing Slowly”, Freesound IDs 590947 and 590950. CC0.
- `window_open.wav` and `window_close.wav`: soundofsong, “door creak 1”, Freesound ID 647646. CC0. The close cue is the recorded creak reversed and shortened for a window.

The game clips are trimmed, leveled, and converted from the Freesound preview audio to mono PCM WAV.
